Create a production-ready 4 columns by 3 rows sprite atlas of twelve seamless,
tileable top-down terrain textures for the existing stylized medieval tower-defense
game. Match the painterly hand-painted style and top-down camera of biomes.png,
medieval-atlas.png, and landmarks.png. Every cell is an exact equal square with no
gutters, borders, labels, or transparency.

Cell order, left to right:
Row 1: forest ground, forest road, autumn ground, autumn road.
Row 2: deadwood ground, deadwood road, drylands ground, drylands road.
Row 3: desert ground, desert road, volcanic ground, volcanic road.

Ground cells use restrained grass, soil, sand, ash, and tiny biome-specific texture
marks. Road cells are related in colour but smoother and more compacted, with faint
ruts, fine grit, and sparse small pebbles. Keep contrast low and detail fine so units,
towers, projectiles, and UI stay readable. Flat orthographic texture only: no focal
objects, large rocks or plants, buildings, bridges, characters, footprints, text,
logos, strong shadows, vignettes, lighting hotspots, seams, or repeated central
motifs. Every cell must tile seamlessly on all four edges.
